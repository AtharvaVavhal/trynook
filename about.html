Mission

One dependable record.

Give independent accommodation operators one dependable record of their property, their residents and their money, so the answer to any question about any of the three is the same wherever it is asked.

Our vision. Make the physical building the interface. A property’s layout, occupancy and availability should be something you look at and understand, not something you reconstruct from a spreadsheet. Choosing a bed online should feel like choosing a seat on a flight, and managing a hundred of them should feel like looking at a floor plan.

The problem

What goes wrong on paper.

PainWhat it looks like in practice
No single source of truth for duesThe register, WhatsApp screenshots and memory disagree, and nobody can prove which is right
Silent revenue leakageA resident skips a month and is never chased because nothing surfaces it
Deposit disputesA year of informal records gets argued over at the door
Manual reconciliationCash, UPI and bank credits are matched by hand every month, for partners who need agreed numbers
Opaque occupancyWhich bed is free, from when, and who had it before lives in memory and on paper

The first test of nook is simple: can the owner answer “who owes what, and why?” faster and more reliably than from the register?

How we build

Principles we hold to.

Money first, and honestly

The financial record has to be right before anything else. Nothing posted is rewritten.

Your words, your rules

nook uses the names and policies your property already has instead of imposing a template.

A human stays in charge

Decisions such as who is admitted stay with people. nook records them, it doesn’t automate them away.

Start small, prove it, then grow

Each stage has to earn the next, and we won’t claim what we haven’t built.

Who it’s for

Independent, owner-run properties.

  • Hostels, PGs and small co-living properties, typically 30 to 300 beds
  • One property or a handful, run by an owner who is also the decision-maker
  • Currently running on paper registers or general-purpose spreadsheets
  • Starting in Pune, where we can work alongside a property in person
