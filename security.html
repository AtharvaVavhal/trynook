Security & privacy

Built to be trusted with your records.

Resident details and rent records are sensitive. Here is how nook is designed to protect them.

In development These are design commitments for the platform as it is built, not certifications.

Principles

Four ideas guide every decision.

Collect less

Every stored detail is a detail that can leak. nook collects only what a named workflow uses, and the pilot does not collect identity documents at all.

Keep organisations apart

One property business must never see another’s residents or finances. Separation is designed in from the first version, not added later.

Never rewrite financial history

Posted records can’t be edited or deleted. Corrections are new records, so every balance can be explained.

Least privilege

People get the access their role needs and nothing more, and every sensitive action leaves a trail.

Separation

Your data stays yours.

nook serves many independent properties from one platform, so isolation matters more than almost anything else.

  • Which organisation you belong to is decided by the server, never by something sent from the browser
  • Asking for another organisation’s record gets “not found”, so nothing is revealed about whether it exists
  • Isolation is checked across lists, reports, exports and background jobs, not only single pages
  • Cross-organisation tests must pass before any release

Accounts

Careful with who gets in.

No public sign-up

Accounts are created by the owner or by invitation.

Revocable sessions

Sign-ins can be ended from the server, and sessions are reset when a role changes.

Safe recovery

Password recovery uses single-use, short-lived tokens. There are no security questions.

Permissions on the server

Every action is checked there, and nobody can grant a permission they don’t hold.

Resident privacy

Residents’ details are not public.

  • The public bed map shows layout and availability only. No name, contact, document or balance is reachable from a public page
  • Warden and front-desk roles have no financial or document access by default
  • Sensitive incident records will have restricted access before they are ever built
  • Phone numbers are personal data and are collected for a stated purpose with a defined retention

Records

An audit trail you can rely on.

Append-only history

Who did what, to what, and when. Inventory and stay changes are never overwritten.

Backups you can restore

A backup only counts once a restore has been tested, so that’s the bar nook holds itself to.

Transparency

What we don’t claim.

nook is still being built. It has not yet had an independent security audit or penetration test, and we make no claim of compliance with any specific regulation. We’ll say so when that changes, rather than before.
