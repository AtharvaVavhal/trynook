General

What is nook?

A management platform for hostels, PGs and co-living properties. It keeps one record of your buildings and beds, the people staying in them, the rent they owe and the deposits you hold, with maintenance and reporting around it.

Who is it for?

Independent, owner-operated properties, typically 30 to 300 beds, that currently run on paper registers or spreadsheets.

Is nook available yet?

nook is in development, starting with a pilot property in Pune. If you run a hostel or PG and want to shape what comes next, write to hello@trynook.in.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Staff often work from a corridor, so the staff web app is designed to be usable on a phone, and the spatial map is designed for touch. A separate resident app is a later phase.

How much does it cost?

Pricing hasn’t been announced yet. Get in touch and we’ll tell you where things stand.

Property & inventory

Can I use my own room and bed names?

Yes. If you call a room “Annexe 2”, nook calls it “Annexe 2”. Labels are yours.

Can I manage more than one property?

Yes. The design supports several properties under one owner. Reports roll up for owners, while staff only see the properties they’re assigned to.

What if I re-plan a room that people are living in?

nook refuses destructive changes to occupied inventory and tells you exactly which records are in the way. Nothing is deleted or reassigned automatically.

Can I see who was in a bed last March?

Yes. Assignments are recorded with dates and never overwritten, so a bed’s history can be reconstructed for any past date.

Admissions & allocation

Can residents book a bed instantly?

Not unless the owner turns that on. By default, an enquiry reaches staff, staff decide, and only then is a bed allocated.

Does choosing a bed reserve it?

No. A choice is a preference that travels with the enquiry. The bed stays available to others until an allocation is committed.

Can two residents be given the same bed?

No. Approval locks the bed, re-checks availability on the server and commits in a single step. If two staff approve at once, one succeeds and the other is told why.

Money

Does nook take payments?

No. Residents pay the property directly by cash, UPI or bank transfer. nook records those payments and reconciles them against what was owed. It never holds your money.

What if I record a payment wrongly?

Posted records aren’t edited or deleted. You reverse the entry and record the right one, so the full history stays visible.

Are deposits counted as revenue?

No. A deposit is a liability held separately, and it’s kept out of collection and revenue figures by design.

When is rent charged?

Billing follows each resident’s own admission date. Someone admitted on the 17th is billed on the 17th, not in a month-end batch.

Privacy & security

Who can see resident information?

Only people whose role needs it, within the properties they’re assigned to. Wardens and front desk have no financial or document access by default.

Does nook store identity documents?

The pilot doesn’t collect or upload identity documents.

Can other hostels see my data?

No. Each organisation’s data is kept separate, and a request for another organisation’s record gets “not found”.

Has nook been security audited?

Not yet. nook is still being built and hasn’t had an independent audit or penetration test. We’ll say so when that changes. See Security & privacy.
