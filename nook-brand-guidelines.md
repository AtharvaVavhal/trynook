---

## 1. The identity

nook is a **wordmark-led** identity. The primary logo is the lowercase wordmark.
The standalone symbol is the **lowercase `n` monogram**, drawn from the identical
vector path as the wordmark's own `n` — not a redrawn lookalike.

Every asset in this package is derived from one approved geometry:

| Construction value | Units |
|---|---|
| x-height | 100 |
| Stroke weight (monoline) | 16 |
| `o` outer diameter | 104 (2-unit optical overshoot) |
| `o` counter diameter | 72 |
| `k` ascender above x-height | 40 |
| `k` arm and leg angle | 45° symmetric |
| Wordmark ink box | 394.32 × 142 |
| Symbol ink box | 80 × 100 |

Letterforms are closed vector paths. No font is referenced, embedded or required.

### Tracking
Optical, not metric. Round-to-round is tighter than straight-to-round:

- `n` → `o` : 10 units
- `o` → `o` : 10 units
- `o` → `k` : 8 units

## 2. Colour

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Indigo | `#171338` | Primary wordmark on light backgrounds |
| Nook Violet | `#7657F6` | Accent; primary app icon ground |
| Soft Lavender | `#F4F1FF` | Surface / panel tint |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed wordmark on dark backgrounds |
| Primary Text | `#26233D` | Body copy (not a logo colour) |

The logo must remain effective in a **single solid colour**. Colour never carries
meaning in the mark.

### Verified contrast (WCAG 2.1)

| Pair | Ratio | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Indigo on White | 17.70:1 | AA / AAA |
| White on Midnight Indigo | 17.70:1 | AA / AAA |
| White `n` on Nook Violet (app icon) | 4.70:1 | AA |
| Midnight Indigo on Soft Lavender | 15.90:1 | AA / AAA |
| Nook Violet on White | 4.70:1 | AA |

## 3. Clear space

Minimum clear space on all four sides = **2× the stroke weight** (32 units, or
0.32 × x-height). Nothing may enter this zone — no type, rule, image edge or
container corner.

For the symbol, the same 32-unit rule applies, measured from the monogram's
ink box.

---

## 4. Minimum size

| Asset | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 16px height (≈44px wide) | 24px and above |
| Symbol / monogram | 16px square | 20px and above |
| App icon | 29px (iOS smallest) | — |

Below 16px the `o` counters begin to close. Use the symbol, not the wordmark,
in any slot under 16px.

The 16px favicon is pixel-tuned: it is full-bleed (no corner radius) with a
larger glyph ratio so the stroke resolves at that size. 32px and above use the
standard rounded tile.

## 6. The violet period

`nook.` is a **marketing-only** variant. It is approved for campaign artwork,
social posts and title cards. It is **not** used for the product UI, the app
icon, the favicon, invoices, receipts, email signatures or any lockup below
32px.

---

## 7. Incorrect use

Do not:

1. Stretch, condense or otherwise distort the proportions.
2. Rotate or set the wordmark on an angle.
3. Recolour the mark outside the approved palette.
4. Place it on a background that drops contrast below 4.5:1.
5. Add gradients, shadows, glows, strokes, outlines or 3D effects.
6. Rebuild the wordmark by typing "nook" in a font — the letterforms are custom.
7. Alter the tracking, or separate the letters.
8. Add a tagline, descriptor or locality inside the primary lockup.
9. Use a house, bed, roof, key or location-pin icon alongside or instead of
   the monogram.
10. Reduce the wordmark below 16px, or crowd the clear space.
