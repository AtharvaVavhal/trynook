What nook does.

Built for independent hostels, PGs and co-living properties that run on paper and spreadsheets today.

  • Your property, as a floor plan

    Buildings, floors, rooms and individual beds, shown as an interactive map. Occupancy is something you look at, not something you piece together from a register.

  • Pick a bed by where it is

    The corner, the window, the bunk near the stairs. Residents choose a place, not a label like “Bed 203C”.

  • Every rupee explained

    Each balance traces line by line from first charge to last payment. Posted records are never edited; corrections are reversals.

  • Residents and stays

    Admissions, bed allocation, room moves and checkout, with the full history of every stay kept.

  • The right access for each role

    Owners, managers, cashiers and wardens see what they need and nothing more.

  • Ready for many properties

    Each organisation’s data is kept separate from day one, so nook can serve many independent properties safely.

The building is the interface.

How it works.

  1. 01

    Map the property

    Set up buildings, floors, rooms and beds once. Everything else builds on that.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Know who owes what

    Record cash, UPI and bank payments against the right charge and see every balance and why.

Questions.

What is nook?

A management platform for hostels, PGs and co-living properties. It keeps one record of your buildings and beds, the people staying in them, the rent they owe and the deposits you hold, with maintenance and reporting around it.

Who is it for?

Independent, owner-operated properties, typically 30 to 300 beds, that currently run on paper registers or spreadsheets.

Does nook take payments?

Not at first. Residents pay the property directly by cash, UPI or bank transfer. nook records and reconciles those payments; it never holds your money.

Is nook available yet?

nook is in development, starting with a pilot property in Pune. If you run a hostel or PG and want to shape what comes next, we’d like to hear from you at hello@trynook.in.

Is resident information kept private?

Yes. nook is designed so that no resident name, contact, document or balance is reachable through any public page.
