The problem

Three records that never agree.

Most independent hostels and PGs run on a paper register, a WhatsApp thread full of UPI screenshots and the owner’s memory. When they disagree, nobody can prove which one is right.

  • No single answer to “who owes what?”

    Dues live in a register, in chat screenshots and in someone’s head, so a missed month can go unchased.

  • Hours of month-end reconciliation

    Cash, UPI and bank credits are matched by hand, and partners still have no books they can both trust.

  • Occupancy is a guess

    Which bed is free, from when, and who had it before is reconstructed from memory and paper.

    Who uses nook

    Built around the people who run a property.

    • Owner / partner

      Occupancy and dues at a glance, and books both partners can agree on.

    • Property manager

      Admissions, bed allocation and arrears follow-up without leaving the page.

    • Cashier

      Record each payment against the right charge, issue a receipt, reconcile.

    • Warden / front desk

      Who is in which bed, and day-to-day tasks, with no finance access by default.

    • Prospective resident

      See what the property is really like and which beds are free. Later

    How it works.

    1. 01

      Map the property

      Set up buildings, floors, rooms and beds once. Everything else builds on that.

    2. 02

      Admit and allocate

      Place residents in real beds. Availability is re-checked when you confirm, so two people never get the same bed.

    3. 03

      Know who owes what

      Record cash, UPI and bank payments against the right charge and see every balance and why.

    Rollout

    Starting small, on purpose.

    Money has to be right before anything else. nook is rolled out in stages, and each stage has to prove itself before the next begins. No dates are promised.

    1. Foundation

      Core platform, property inventory and financial correctness, verified independently.

    2. Pilot

      One hostel in Pune with a small first group of residents and a full billing cycle.

    3. Growing the pilot

      Expand bed by bed as the evidence supports it.

    4. More properties

      Open nook to other independent hostels and PGs.

    Questions.

    What is nook?

    A management platform for hostels, PGs and co-living properties. It keeps one record of your buildings and beds, the people staying in them, the rent they owe and the deposits you hold, with maintenance and reporting around it.

    Who is it for?

    Independent, owner-operated properties, typically 30 to 300 beds, that currently run on paper registers or spreadsheets.

    Does nook take payments?

    Not at first. Residents pay the property directly by cash, UPI or bank transfer. nook records and reconciles those payments; it never holds your money.

    Is nook available yet?

    nook is in development, starting with a pilot property in Pune. If you run a hostel or PG and want to shape what comes next, we’d like to hear from you at hello@trynook.in.

    Is resident information kept private?

    nook is designed so that no resident name, contact, document or balance is reachable through any public page. Read how we approach security and privacy.
